### Subtitle (170 chars max)
Restaurant management suite: POS, analytics, inventory & team

### Full Description (4000 chars max)
Mise is a complete restaurant management system designed for modern hospitality businesses. Manage cash, staff, inventory, and guest orders from a single app.

**5 integrated modules:**

• **Manager** — Daily cash reconciliation, employee tracking, expense categories, and commission management
• **Analytics** — Real-time revenue tracking, profit analysis, forecasts, and staff payroll insights
• **Stash** — Inventory management, stock movements, low-stock alerts, and inventory audits
• **People** — Staff scheduling, attendance tracking, task management, checklists, and pay records
• **Menu** — QR code ordering, table management, kitchen integration, and order tracking

**Key features:**
✓ Multi-currency support (EUR, USD, and more)
✓ Role-based access (owner, manager, staff)
✓ Full localization (8 languages)
✓ Offline resilience
✓ Real-time team collaboration
✓ Detailed financial reports

Perfect for restaurants, cafes, hookah bars, and hospitality venues. Works on iPhone and iPad.
